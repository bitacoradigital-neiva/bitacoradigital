# Product

## Platform

web

## Users
Two audiences, served equally (confirmed 2026-11-11):
- Small businesses and entrepreneurs in Neiva (Huila) and the rest of Colombia who want to look professional online and get clients through WhatsApp.
- Individuals in Neiva whose personal computer is slow, infected or needs a clean reinstall.

Most visitors arrive on a phone. They are deciding whether this new, unknown provider is trustworthy and affordable before writing on WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose
Bitácora Digital sells three services as one technology solution, with equal weight: computer support (optimization, formatting with official drivers, remote support), websites (landing pages, turnkey sites with domain/hosting/SSL, corporate sites), and brand design (advertising pieces, visual identity kits). Success = the visitor builds a quote in the on-page cotizador and opens WhatsApp, or writes directly.

## Positioning
A one-person local provider that makes technology uncomplicated: transparent prices visible before any contact, free initial diagnosis, backup of the client's files before formatting, 50% advance / balance on delivery for web and design, delivery from 24 h. Physical service in Neiva; web, design and remote support for all of Colombia.

## Operating Context
- Contact and sales happen on WhatsApp (+57 314 432 1446); the cotizador composes the WhatsApp message.
- Payments: cash in Neiva, Nequi, Daviplata, Bancolombia transfer.
- Delivery times: basic setup 24 h; full formatting 24–48 h; landing page 3–5 days; full site 7–15 days.
- Prices in COP with a USD toggle (see servicesList in index.html); final value confirmed after diagnosis.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Static HTML/CSS/JS, single index.html (+ privacidad.html, terminos.html), deployed on Vercel from GitHub repo bitacoradigital-neiva/bitacoradigital, branch gou.
- Must be fast and look right on low/mid-range phones; heavy motion must degrade by device tier.
- Legal pages (terms, privacy) and consent checkbox before sending a quote must stay.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Bitácora Digital. Logo: blue pixel "B" with a cursor arrow and trailing pixel squares (img/logo-*.webp). Brand blue.
- Voice: plain Spanish (Colombia), direct, no technical jargon ("Tecnología y diseño. Sin complicaciones.").
- Brand only: no personal name or face on the site (confirmed 2026-11-11).

## Evidence on Hand
- No clients, testimonials, case studies or real photos of work yet. Never fabricate any of them.
- Assets: logo files; laptop-opening frame sequence (img/laptop), 3D monitor sequence (img/monitor), stock loops from Pixabay/Pexels/Mixkit (video/), robot loop, service card images (img/cot).

## Product Principles
1. Price and process visible before contact: trust comes from transparency, not claims.
2. One clear next step everywhere: build a quote or write on WhatsApp.
3. Phone first: every effect must earn its cost on a mid-range phone.
4. Honest proof only: show method, guarantees and the brand's own craft, never invented social proof.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Respect prefers-reduced-motion and reduced transparency; readable contrast; keyboard-usable cotizador and dialogs.
